# The Unreal City of London

London supplies a precise walking map: London Bridge, King William Street, Saint Mary Woolnoth, the Strand, and the streets beside the Thames. These are workplaces, roads, churches, and commercial meeting places. Their specificity gives the shifting voices a shared material setting.

Yet the “Unreal City” is simultaneously Baudelaire's haunted modern city and Dante's realm of the dead. Crowds move on the clock without appearing fully alive to one another. Later, music and the interior of Magnus Martyr interrupt this estrangement. London is therefore more than a single emblem of corruption: its streets contain brief possibilities of attention alongside commerce, routine, and isolation.
