# That Shakespearian Rag

“That Shakespearian Rag” was published in 1912, with music by Dave Stamper and words by Gene Buck and Herman Ruby. Its chorus advertises Shakespeare through the language of elegance and intelligence. The [surviving sheet music](https://yorkspace.library.yorku.ca/items/62165c1c-bb33-4b9a-9c6b-6992f4546e08) records the song and its authors.

The tune intrudes just after the remembered line about pearls from *The Tempest*. Shakespeare reaches the troubled room through both a haunting dramatic quotation and a commercial song. The rag's compulsive brightness can be heard as a defence against the demand to speak, or as another borrowed voice occupying a mind that cannot answer directly.
