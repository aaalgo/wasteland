# Thomas Kyd's The Spanish Tragedy

Eliot's note identifies Thomas Kyd's *The Spanish Tragedy* as the source of the closing appeal to Hieronymo, the play's bereaved father and revenger, usually named Hieronimo in editions of the drama. His apparent madness develops beside a deliberate plot to avenge his murdered son.

The play ends through a performance that turns into real killing, using theatrical roles and different languages to conceal its purpose. Beside Eliot's gathered fragments, the allusion makes the making of art inseparable from grief, violence, and damaged speech. The reassuring sound of “Why then Ile fit you” carries a threatening dramatic history.
