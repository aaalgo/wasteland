# London Bridge Is Falling Down

The nursery song about London Bridge falling down enters the closing sequence after the speaker asks whether he can set his lands in order. Its repetitive, familiar rhythm reduces the great city's collapse to a piece of communal childhood memory.

Earlier, London Bridge carries the crowd whom the poem sees through Dante's dead. Near the end, the bridge itself becomes a fragment of remembered song. The return links public infrastructure, historical ruin, and language handed down without a stable explanation. The poem quotes the refrain; it does not establish any particular theory about the rhyme's historical origins.
