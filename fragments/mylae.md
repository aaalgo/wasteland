# Stetson and the Battle of Mylae

Mylae was a naval battle between Rome and Carthage in 260 BCE, during the First Punic War. The speaker greets the modern-sounding Stetson as a former shipmate there, collapsing a vast interval of historical time inside a London street encounter.

The impossible reunion makes collective memory behave like personal recollection. Ancient warfare, the recent experience of mass death, and the question about a planted corpse enter the same conversation. Stetson need not be decoded as one historical individual: the anachronism makes the persistence of war and death more forceful than a realistic identification would.
