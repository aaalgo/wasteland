# Shantih: Peace

Eliot's final note identifies the threefold “Shantih” as a formal ending to an Upanishad. The Sanskrit word means peace; he suggests the Christian phrase about peace passing understanding while warning that it is an inadequate rendering.

After the quotations, ruins, and thunder's three commands, the poem takes on the cadence of a closing invocation. Whether this peace has been attained remains open. It can be read as a prayer or an act of utterance that seeks the stillness the preceding voices could not secure.
