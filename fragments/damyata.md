# Damyata: Control Yourself

“Damyata” means “control yourselves” or “exercise self-restraint.” In the thunder fable of the *Brihadaranyaka Upanishad* 5.2, the gods understand Prajāpati's syllable “Da” as this command. Eliot renders it “control” in his note and places it after giving and sympathy in his own sequence. See [the Upanishad's three commands](https://www.baps.org/Article/2011/The-Bruhadaranyaka-Upanishad-2024.aspx).

The answering boat turns discipline into an image of responsive movement. It runs gaily under an expert hand, unlike the drifting or wrecked vessels elsewhere in the poem. The heart's response is phrased conditionally—“would have responded”—so this harmony is imagined or missed as well as offered. Control here can be read as a condition of willing response, though the language of obedience keeps its emotional tension.
