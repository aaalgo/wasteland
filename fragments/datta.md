# Datta: Give

“Datta” means “give.” In the *Brihadaranyaka Upanishad* 5.2, human beings understand the syllable “Da” as a command to give, while gods and demons hear other instructions suited to their failings. Eliot moves giving to the beginning of his three responses to the thunder. See [the Upanishad's three commands](https://www.baps.org/Article/2011/The-Bruhadaranyaka-Upanishad-2024.aspx).

The poem asks what has actually been given and answers through a moment of surrender that ordinary records cannot preserve. Obituaries, remembered reputations, and legal papers register a life without containing its decisive inward act. Giving becomes an exposure of the self that prudence cannot undo, rather than simply an amount of property transferred.
