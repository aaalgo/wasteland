# The Pub's Last Call

“HURRY UP PLEASE IT'S TIME” is the publican's closing call, repeatedly interrupting the conversation about Lil and Albert. The words belong to the ordinary regulation of a drinking evening. Their capital letters and recurrence make them sound increasingly impersonal beside the intimate disclosures they cut across.

The refrain also lends the scene an undertone of mortality and approaching judgment. Time runs out while the speakers discuss worn bodies, unwanted pregnancies, and the obligations of marriage. No supernatural speaker needs to be supplied: the pressure comes from hearing an everyday formula acquire meanings its immediate speaker may never intend.
