# The Chapel Perilous

Eliot names the approach to the Chapel Perilous, as discussed in Jessie L. Weston's *From Ritual to Romance*, among the themes of Part V. In Grail romance, a dangerous chapel can test the questing knight through an encounter with death, terror, and apparently supernatural obstacles.

Here the chapel is empty, its door swinging in the wind. The assurance that dry bones cannot harm anyone seems to drain the ordeal of its terrors, yet also leaves the sacred building deserted. The cock's cry, lightning, and damp gust mark a change in the scene. They suggest the possibility of renewal without narrating a completed Grail quest or a restored community.
