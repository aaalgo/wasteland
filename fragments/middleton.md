# Thomas Middleton and Chess

The section title can recall Thomas Middleton's *A Game at Chess*, but Eliot's explicit note identifies a different Middleton play: *Women Beware Women*. In its chess scene, a game helps distract a watching woman while Bianca is exposed to the Duke's sexual pursuit. The distinction matters: the documented allusion is to the dramatic situation in *Women Beware Women*.

Chess brings calculation, restricted movement, and unequal knowledge into the room where the poem's couple cannot communicate. Their proposed game offers a way to occupy time without resolving fear or desire. Read beside Middleton's scene, apparently civilised leisure carries the possibility of manipulation and sexual coercion.
